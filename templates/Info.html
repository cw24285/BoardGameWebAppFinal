The bayview board game day is held at the Bayview Community Centre at 72 Bayview Road, Bayview, Auckland. It is on the 2nd Saturday of every month from 12pm-4pm. Feel free to bring the whole family. Let the kids run around while you play some games, or they can join in with us!

We have lots of different board games, from long to short, easy to hard, work together or fight each other. For the games you like a bit too much there are opportunities to take them home and play again with your family, just make sure to bring them back.

If the harder more complicated board games are your style or you have a grudge against children then there is an adult board game night held at the same place every last Friday of each month at 7pm.
